City and building sprites rendered from the Hexagon Kit by Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl),
licensed under Creative Commons Zero (CC0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were rendered in isometric view with tools/IsoSprites (in conquerTEST, the other version of the game); the airfield is built from Kenney's
grass tile with a runway, a control tower, a hangar and a plane made of boxes.

The unit, ship and aircraft sprites in Units/ are made for this game: modeled in code in
tools/IsoSprites/Units.cs, in the style of Kenney's kits, and rendered with the same tool
("units" mode). Each name-team.png holds, in grey, the parts the game paints in the country's color.
